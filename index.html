Projects

Accounting fraud detection: replicating a Journal of Accounting Research result

An out-of-time replication of Bao et al. (2020) on 146,045 firm-years: RUSBoost reaches AUC 0.715 against the paper's corrected 0.723 and beats the Beneish M-score (0.563), but its top-1% advantage does not replicate. Letting the same fraud case into training and test inflates AUC to 0.778, the leakage a 2021 critique found.

Deep hedging: can a neural network hedge an option better than Black-Scholes delta?

Under Heston volatility and 20 bps trading costs, a network trained to minimise expected shortfall cuts the 95% tail loss of a short call 10–13% versus delta hedging and beats a Whalley-Wilmott no-trade band, at the price of a wider P&L spread. Tested on 200,000 fresh paths; with no costs it recovers delta (correlation 0.997).

Code

Filings copilot: 10-K answers with citations, and a guardrail that checks them

A fully local system answers questions about five companies' 10-Ks and cites the passage. On 53 known-answer questions it is right 84% of the time and says "not found" on all 10 unanswerable ones; the groundedness guardrail from our SSRN paper catches 6 of 7 wrong answers, at the cost of holding back 14 correct ones.

Code

Does the tone of an earnings release move the stock? FinBERT vs a finance dictionary

On 2,482 earnings releases from 75 large caps (2018–2026), FinBERT's change in tone predicts the two-day reaction (+61 bp per standard deviation, t = 4.58), while the Loughran-McDonald dictionary predicts nothing. After day two the effect is gone: an out-of-sample long-short loses money after costs.

Harbor Terminal: a terminal-style market workstation on free data

Keyboard commands like TJX DES, TJX FA, ECO and TJX AI pull SEC filings, FRED macro data and prices. A local language model writes a five-bullet briefing, and every number it writes is checked against the source data (21 of 21 matched on the recorded run). 62 offline tests.

What is TJX worth? A DCF built from SEC filings, in an auditable Excel model

Gordon-growth and exit-multiple DCFs both value TJX near $106 a share, about 25% below the $140.20 market price on October 27, 2026. The price implies a 5.88% WACC or a 14.5% EBIT margin. The workbook has 474 live formulas, checked against the Python model on every run.

SEC fundamentals warehouse for 23 Massachusetts companies

A DuckDB star schema built from 608,414 raw XBRL facts, with SQL data-quality tests and a restatement audit (2,334 values changed after first filing). DuPont analysis shows American Tower's 72% ROE comes mostly from leverage, while TJX earns 59% by turning its assets over 1.79 times a year.

Code

Portfolio Risk Lab: do optimised portfolios beat simple ones, and which VaR model survives 2008?

Walk-forward backtest, Dec 2007 to Sep 2026, net of costs: Max Sharpe led on Sharpe (0.63 vs 0.58 for 60/40), but no strategy beat equal weight or 60/40 significantly. Filtered historical simulation was the only 99% VaR model to pass the Kupiec test and stay out of the Basel red zone.

Code

Credit risk scorecard: can a transparent bank scorecard match gradient boosting?

On 9,000 held-out credit-card accounts, an 11-variable points scorecard reaches AUC 0.764 against 0.781 for gradient boosting, with almost all of the gap among the safest accounts. Includes cutoff economics, reason codes, and a fairness check that flags age-band disparities.

Code

Recession probability from the yield curve, tested out of sample

A term-spread probit refit every year since 1985 ranks months well (AUC 0.874) but does not beat the base rate on Brier score, because of the 2022–24 inversion. Credit and labour signals added nothing. September 2026 reading: 11.8%.
